## Note on the two surviving mutants (added by hand, not by `tb/mutation.py`)

Regenerating this file with `python tb/mutation.py --out results/verification/mutants.md` replaces the table above and drops this note.

M1i and D2 both weaken the weight-drain wait and survive only in the int8 double-buffered build (`int8-db`). M1i shortens the int8 drain by one cycle; D2 never arms the drain counter of the second weight bank. In that build a weight bank is reused only two tiles later, and the four weight beats of the tile in between always take longer than the three-cycle drain, so the wait can never stall anything: by this argument, neither bug can change behaviour, and both are argued to be equivalent mutants in this build. Both mutants are caught in every build where the drain matters (M1i in `int8-base` and `int8-perf`, D2 in `ternary-db`).
